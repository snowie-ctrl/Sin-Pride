# Pride

*"Where do I stand?"*

Pride is a low-effort health and fitness tracker, themed on the sin it is named after and built as the sibling of
[Gluttony](https://github.com/snowie-ctrl/Sin-Gluttony). It is for an everyday person or a beginner: plan
routines and splits, log what you did, see your progress, and get gentle suggestions for when to step up. It is
not a medical app and never tells anyone how they must eat or train.

It is a personal project in several parts. Nothing is built yet: the design is in `docs/superpowers/specs/`.

| Part | What it is | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Training | Exercise catalog, guided start, programs in rotation, workout log, progress, progression suggestions (desktop) | Designed |
| 2. Gym companion | The iOS app that runs a workout set by set | Planned |
| 3. Nutrition and body | Macro targets, food logging, body weight | Planned |
| 4. The watcher | Gentle, rule-based nudges | Planned |
| 5. Through the door | Linking Gluttony: meals made there, recipe suggestions to meet targets | Planned |
